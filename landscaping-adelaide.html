Adelaide's Local Landscaping Specialists

When it comes to landscaping in Adelaide, working with a local team makes all the difference. At Maddern's Place, we understand Adelaide's soil conditions, seasonal patterns, and the unique challenges that different suburbs present — from the clay-heavy ground throughout Hackham and Morphett Vale to the coastal conditions in Hallett Cove and the sloped blocks that are common right across the southern suburbs.

Our landscaping services cover everything your outdoor space could need: retaining walls, new turf installation, irrigation systems, drainage, paving, planting, and gutter cleaning. We manage projects from the initial site visit through to completion, handling every part of the work in-house so your project runs on schedule and on budget without the handoffs and delays that come from using multiple contractors.

We work with homeowners, property investors, and builders across Adelaide's south, consistently delivering high-quality outdoor spaces that are built to last in local conditions. Honest quotes, clear communication, and results that we're proud of — that's the standard we hold every job to.

What We Do

Our Complete Landscaping Services

🧱

Retaining Walls

Block and timber retaining walls designed for Adelaide's sloped blocks. Properly engineered with integrated drainage — built to last decades without maintenance issues.

🌿

Turf Installation

Fresh Sir Walter, Kikuyu, and TifTuf turf supplied and installed with full ground preparation — so your new lawn establishes quickly and stays healthy through Adelaide's summers.

💧

Irrigation Systems

New system design and installation using Hunter MP Rotators and Bluetooth NODE timers — designed for Adelaide's watering day restrictions and clay-heavy soils.

🔩

Drainage Solutions

Surface and subsurface drainage to manage Adelaide's heavy winter rainfall — protecting your home, garden, and landscaping investment from water damage and soil erosion.

🌱

Planting & Mulching

Plant selection suited to Adelaide's climate — native and exotic species chosen for the local conditions, finished with quality mulch for a water-wise, low-maintenance garden.

💨

Gutter Cleaning

Vacuum gutter cleaning service keeping Adelaide homes protected from water damage caused by blocked gutters and overflowing downpipes after autumn leaf fall.
