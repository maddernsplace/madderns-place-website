What We Offer

Keep Your Home Protected with Clean Gutters

Blocked gutters are one of the most overlooked risks to a home. When gutters fill with leaves, debris, and sediment, water backs up under roofing, saturates fascias, and causes costly damage to walls, foundations, and ceilings. At Maddern's Place, we provide a thorough, professional gutter vacuum cleaning service across Adelaide's southern suburbs.

Unlike manual raking methods, our vacuum cleaning system removes all fine debris, soil, and seed material — not just the large leaves. This leaves your gutters completely clear and flowing freely, with no mess left behind on your roof or in your garden.

We service homes across Hackham, Morphett Vale, Hallett Cove, Reynella, Christies Beach, and the surrounding southern Adelaide suburbs. Whether you're a homeowner needing an annual clean or a property manager maintaining multiple properties, we deliver a reliable, professional service every time.

Local Knowledge

Why Adelaide Homes Need Regular Gutter Cleaning

Adelaide's southern suburbs experience a distinct seasonal pattern — heavy leaf fall in autumn, storm activity in winter, and dry summers that bake debris into tight, compacted clumps. This cycle means gutters in areas like Morphett Vale and Hackham can go from clear to critically blocked in a matter of weeks during peak autumn.

Homes with large eucalyptus, peppercorn, or deciduous trees nearby are particularly at risk. Even properties with relatively few trees can accumulate enough wind-blown debris to cause overflows and water damage if gutters aren't cleaned at least once per year — and twice yearly for properties with heavy tree coverage.

Our gutter vacuum system is specifically well-suited to Adelaide's conditions. It handles the fine, compacted debris that manual cleaning leaves behind, and it's safe for all gutter profiles including quad, half-round, and box gutters. The vacuum collects everything in one pass rather than blowing debris onto your garden or back into the gutter.

Regular gutter maintenance is one of the most cost-effective ways to protect your home. A blocked gutter can lead to roof cavity water ingress, internal ceiling leaks, and wall damage that costs thousands to repair — all preventable with a routine annual clean. It's a small investment that makes a big difference to the longevity of your home.
