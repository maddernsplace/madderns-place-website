Aldinga Beach Irrigation & Kikuyu Lawn Installation

About This Project

The Project

This landscaping project in Aldinga Beach transformed a bare and underutilised backyard into a practical, low-maintenance outdoor space designed for healthy lawn growth and efficient water management.

By combining premium Kikuyu turf, a fully automated Hunter irrigation system, metal garden edging, and screened tree mulch, the property now features a clean and functional landscape that will continue to improve as the lawn establishes.

The Goal

The existing yard lacked usable lawn space and required significant preparation before landscaping could commence. The homeowners wanted a durable lawn solution that would thrive in Adelaide's climate while minimising ongoing maintenance and maximising water efficiency.

The primary objectives included:

  • Establishing a healthy new lawn area
  • Installing an automated irrigation system
  • Improving water distribution and efficiency
  • Creating clean landscape edges
  • Reducing future maintenance requirements
  • Enhancing the overall appearance and usability of the backyard

The Process

The project began with excavation and removal of the existing surface material to create the correct levels throughout the yard.

Once excavation was complete, the site was carefully prepared and graded to ensure proper drainage and provide an ideal foundation for turf establishment.

A fully automated irrigation system was then installed using a Hunter Node battery-operated controller paired with Hunter MP Rotator sprinklers. This system was designed to provide efficient and even water coverage across the entire lawn area while reducing water waste.

Following irrigation installation, metal garden edging was installed to create clean separation between lawn and landscape areas. Screened tree mulch was then applied to garden beds to improve moisture retention and provide a neat finished appearance.

The project was completed with the installation of premium Kikuyu turf, creating a durable and hard-wearing lawn well suited to the conditions experienced throughout Aldinga Beach and the southern Adelaide region.

The Result

The completed Aldinga Beach landscaping project transformed an empty and uneven backyard into a functional outdoor space that is both attractive and easy to maintain.

The combination of premium Kikuyu turf and Hunter irrigation provides the ideal foundation for a healthy, long-lasting lawn, while the metal edging and mulched garden beds create clean lines and a professional finish throughout the landscape.

With efficient irrigation, improved drainage, and a durable turf selection suited to Adelaide's climate, the homeowners now have a practical outdoor area that will continue to mature and improve over time.
