Garden Makeovers That Stick

Adelaide's southern suburbs have thousands of gardens that were once someone's pride and joy but have gradually fallen into decline — overgrown shrubs, dead lawns, collapsed garden beds, and ageing retaining walls that no longer do their job. For many homeowners in Morphett Vale, Woodcroft, Christie Downs, and Reynella, the garden has become a source of stress rather than enjoyment. That's where a complete garden makeover makes all the difference.

A Maddern's Place garden makeover is a managed transformation from current state to finished result. We don't just do the pleasant planting work and leave the hard parts for you — we handle the full scope, from initial site assessment and design, through demolition and clearance of what's not working, to all the hard landscaping elements like retaining walls, paving, and drainage, and finally the soft landscaping of new turf, garden beds, and irrigation.

The makeover process starts with an honest assessment. We look at what can be retained — established trees, good-condition structures, healthy plants worth keeping — and what needs to go. Retaining mature elements not only preserves their value but can dramatically reduce project cost and give the new garden an established feel from day one. We then develop a plan that addresses the functional issues (drainage, levels, access) before the aesthetic ones.

For front yard makeovers, street appeal is a key consideration. We design front gardens that improve the relationship between house and street, using levels, planting, and surfacing to create a welcoming, well-maintained presentation. For backyard transformations, functionality drives the design — usable lawn, comfortable entertaining space, low-maintenance planting, and good irrigation to keep it all alive through Adelaide's fierce summer heat.

Garden Makeovers Adelaide South — FAQs

Timeline depends on scope. A front yard makeover including clearing, new turf, edging, and planting typically takes two to four days. A full backyard transformation with retaining walls, paving, lawn, irrigation, and planting might take two to four weeks. We provide a clear project timeline with every quote so you know what to expect and can plan around the work.
We handle the landscaping elements directly — clearing, earthworks, turf, retaining walls, planting, and irrigation are all done by our own team. For elements outside our scope such as electrical work for garden lighting, we coordinate with trusted local tradespeople. You deal with us as the single point of contact throughout the project.
Absolutely. Many garden makeovers involve selective clearance — removing what isn't working while retaining established trees, hedges, or plantings that have value. We assess what's worth keeping and design around it, which can significantly reduce both cost and establishment time compared to a full-clearance approach.
Low maintenance is a priority for most of our southern Adelaide clients — and it's entirely achievable with the right plant selections, good mulching, and an appropriate irrigation system. Australian natives and Mediterranean species that suit Adelaide's climate require less water and intervention once established. We design with your available time and maintenance preferences in mind from the start.
Garden makeover costs vary widely depending on scope, size, and elements involved. A front yard refresh with new turf, edging, and some planting differs significantly from a comprehensive backyard transformation including retaining walls, paving, and irrigation. We provide detailed, itemised quotes so you have full cost clarity before committing to any work.
