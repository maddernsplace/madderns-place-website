What We Offer

Irrigation That Works Every Time

Adelaide summers are among the hottest in Australia, and SA Water's watering restrictions mean every watering window counts. A properly designed and installed irrigation system is the difference between a lawn and garden that thrives through the heat and one that struggles or dies back in summer, requiring expensive renovation or re-turfing each autumn.

We design, supply, and install complete irrigation systems for residential properties across Hackham, Morphett Vale, Hallett Cove, Reynella, and the wider Adelaide south. Our systems are designed around the specific conditions of your property — the layout, the soil type, the plant types in different areas, the available water pressure, and your watering schedule restrictions. We use Hunter products across most installations, specifically Hunter MP Rotator heads for lawns and Hunter NODE Bluetooth timers, which allow easy scheduling adjustment from a phone without needing to dig up the controller.

We also repair and upgrade existing systems. An old, poorly adjusted system can be as costly as no system — broken heads spray water in the wrong direction, blocked emitters leave plants dry while the timer runs, and pressure problems cause premature component failure. We diagnose systematically and fix what's actually causing the problem rather than just replacing parts.

Step by Step

Our Irrigation Installation Process

A properly designed system takes planning before installation begins. Here's how we approach every new irrigation install.

1

Site Assessment & Watering Audit

We walk the full property, measure the areas to be irrigated, assess water pressure at the tap, identify any existing pipe or system components, and note sun exposure, plant types, and soil conditions. This information determines the system design — number of zones, head types, pipe sizing, and controller requirements. Getting this right upfront prevents having to revisit the design once installation begins.

2

System Design & Head Placement Plan

We map out the pipe routes, zone breaks, head positions, and controller location. Zones are designed so each zone covers plants with similar water needs — lawn areas run separately from garden beds, shaded areas separately from full-sun areas. This allows each zone to be programmed with the right run time, rather than compromising some areas to avoid overwatering others. We also design with SA Water's watering schedule in mind, ensuring the system can deliver adequate water within the allowed window.

3

Pipe Trench Excavation & Routing

Poly pipe is buried to protect it from UV degradation and mowing damage. We trench at the appropriate depth for each area, route cleanly around garden beds and structures, and minimise surface disruption. Where trenching would disturb established plantings, we can use narrow-profile tools or bore under pathways rather than cutting through them.

4

Head & Emitter Installation

Sprinkler heads, drip emitters, or micro-spray heads are installed at the planned positions. Hunter MP Rotator heads are our preferred choice for lawn zones — they operate at low pressure, apply water slowly enough to avoid run-off on clay soils, and have excellent distribution uniformity. For garden bed zones we use inline drip tubing or micro-sprays depending on plant density and spacing.

5

Controller Wiring & Zone Setup

Solenoid valves are wired to the controller and each zone is tested for activation, coverage, and any adjustments needed. For most residential jobs we install Hunter NODE Bluetooth battery-operated timers — they don't need mains power or hard wiring, allow scheduling adjustment from a phone via the Hunter Home Court app, and can be placed anywhere on the property. For larger systems we use wired multi-zone controllers.

6

Test, Adjust & Program

Every zone is run in full and observed for coverage, pressure, and uniformity. Head arc and radius are adjusted where needed. The controller is programmed with a compliant watering schedule based on SA Water's current restrictions for your property's address. We walk you through the controller so you know how to adjust run times and days when needed, and leave you with written zone information for future reference.
