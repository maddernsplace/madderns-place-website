Local Expertise

Landscapers Who Know Adelaide's South

Maddern's Place Garden & Landscaping was built in the south and stays in the south. Operating from our Hackham base, we understand exactly what southern Adelaide properties need — from the heavy clay soils of Morphett Vale to the sandy-clay mixes in Seaford and the challenging slopes common through Happy Valley and O'Halloran Hill. That local knowledge shapes every decision we make on site.

Our work covers the full spectrum of residential landscaping. We design and build retaining walls to manage difficult slopes, install turf on properly prepared ground, set up efficient irrigation systems, and create planted garden beds that thrive in South Australia's climate. Whether you're starting fresh on a new build or transforming an established but tired backyard, we approach each project as a cohesive outdoor build.

Clay soil is one of the most common challenges in Adelaide's southern suburbs. It holds water poorly in summer and becomes waterlogged in winter, creating drainage problems that undermine lawns and gardens. We address this at the ground level — incorporating drainage solutions, soil amendments, and the right plant selections to work with your site rather than against it.

From McLaren Vale's rural-residential properties to the dense suburban blocks of Christie Downs and Woodcroft, we bring the same level of attention and quality to every project. No subcontracting, no cutting corners — just reliable local tradespeople who take pride in the finished result.
