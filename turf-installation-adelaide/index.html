What We Offer

New Lawns Laid the Right Way

A new lawn is one of the single biggest improvements most Adelaide backyards can get — but the difference between a lawn that thrives and one that struggles for years comes down almost entirely to what happens before the turf arrives. Ground preparation is everything. A lawn laid over poorly prepared soil with inadequate drainage or compacted sub-base will look average for a year and then deteriorate. Done properly, it will look better every season for fifteen years.

We've laid turf across hundreds of properties throughout Hackham, Morphett Vale, Hallett Cove, Reynella, and the wider Adelaide south. We understand the local soil conditions — the dense clay that sits under many properties in this area, the limestone base that can affect drainage in coastal suburbs, and the alkaline pH that influences which varieties perform best. We adjust our ground preparation approach for each site, not just every job.

Our turf installation service covers everything: variety selection advice, site clearing, ground preparation including soil amendment where needed, supply of fresh locally grown turf, laying, and a detailed establishment care guide so you know exactly what your new lawn needs in the first six weeks.

Step by Step

Our Turf Installation Process

We follow a consistent process developed across years of turf laying in Adelaide's south — every step matters for a lawn that establishes quickly and performs long term.

1

Site Assessment & Variety Recommendation

We visit the site, assess soil type, drainage, shade patterns, and how the space is used. From this we recommend the best turf variety for your specific conditions — not just what's popular, but what will actually thrive in your yard. We also identify any drainage issues, level changes, or soil problems that need addressing before turf goes down.

2

Clear, Scalp & Remove Old Lawn

Existing lawn, weeds, and debris are removed completely. For renovations we scalp the existing lawn as close as possible before removal. Leaving old lawn material underneath new turf traps moisture, creates an air gap that stops roots knitting into the soil, and can harbour pests and disease — it all has to go.

3

Ground Preparation & Soil Amendment

The soil is ripped and worked to a depth of at least 80–100mm. Where the soil is heavy clay — as is common throughout Hackham and Morphett Vale — we incorporate gypsum and quality top dress to improve drainage and soil structure. In areas with low pH or compaction, we add appropriate amendments before raking to a level, even surface. This is the most time-consuming part of the job, and the most important.

4

Pre-Turf Fertiliser & Wetting Agent

A slow-release starter fertiliser and soil wetting agent are applied to the prepared surface before turf arrives. Adelaide's soils can be hydrophobic — meaning water beads off rather than penetrating — and pre-treating with a wetting agent gives the new turf the best possible chance of establishing evenly.

5

Lay Fresh Turf & Trim to Fit

Fresh turf is delivered on installation day and laid immediately. We work in staggered brick-pattern rows, ensuring tight seams with no gaps. Edges are cut precisely along pathways, garden bed edges, and fence lines. Turf that sits on a pallet for 24–48 hours before laying has significantly reduced establishment rates — our approach ensures every roll goes down fresh.

6

Roll, Water In & Establishment Guide

Once laid, the turf is rolled to eliminate air pockets and ensure good soil contact across the entire area. We water it in thoroughly on site. Before we leave, we walk you through the establishment watering schedule — covering daily watering frequency for the first two weeks, how to reduce frequency as roots establish, and when to do the first cut. A little care in the first six weeks determines the quality of the lawn for the next decade.

Turf Installation FAQs

Sir Walter Buffalo is our most commonly recommended variety for Adelaide's south. It handles partial shade well, is drought tolerant once established, and copes with Adelaide's clay soils without excessive inputs. Kikuyu is a great choice for full-sun yards that get heavy use — it's aggressive and establishes fast. For very low maintenance requirements, Zoysia is excellent. We'll recommend the right variety for your specific yard during the site assessment.
Pricing depends on the area size, how much ground preparation is required, site access, and the turf variety chosen. Ground prep is often the biggest variable — a yard that needs significant levelling or soil amendment costs more than one that just needs the old lawn scalped off. We provide free on-site quotes with transparent line-item pricing so you know exactly what's included before committing.
Autumn (March–May) is ideal — soil temperatures are still warm enough for good root establishment, but the weather has cooled so the turf isn't under immediate heat stress. Spring (September–November) is also excellent. Summer laying is possible but requires intensive watering to get the turf through the heat before it's fully established. We don't recommend winter laying for warm-season varieties like Sir Walter and Kikuyu as growth slows significantly and establishment takes much longer.
Most varieties become established within 4–6 weeks under normal autumn or spring conditions — meaning the roots have knitted into the soil and the turf can handle normal foot traffic. During this period, consistent watering is critical.
