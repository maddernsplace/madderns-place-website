New Lawns That Actually Establish

The most common reason new turf fails in Adelaide's southern suburbs isn't the turf variety or even the watering regime after installation — it's what happens before the rolls go down. Clay-heavy soils across Morphett Vale, Christie Downs, Woodcroft, and Reynella need amendment, aeration, and careful levelling before they'll accept and hold a new lawn properly. We treat ground preparation as the foundation of every installation, because that's exactly what it is.

We source our turf from South Australian growers and deliver it fresh on installation day. Sir Walter Buffalo remains the standout choice for most southern suburban properties — it handles Adelaide's dry summers on moderate irrigation, tolerates the partial shade that fences and neighbouring trees create, and produces a lush, soft lawn that holds up to family use. For full-sun applications, Kikuyu offers aggressive growth and excellent drought recovery, while Couch varieties suit properties where a finer-textured, low-growing lawn is preferred.

Every turf installation we complete includes removal of the existing surface, rototilling to break up compacted soil, gypsum application to improve clay structure, fine grading to eliminate bumps and hollows, starter fertiliser, and a thorough first watering. For sites with drainage issues — waterlogging is common on heavier soils through the Onkaparinga region — we'll incorporate drainage solutions before the turf goes down rather than hoping the problem resolves itself later.

After installation, we walk you through a care schedule covering the critical first six weeks — when to water, how much, when to apply the first post-install fertiliser, and when you can start mowing. Getting that establishment phase right is what converts a professional installation into a lawn that will last for years.

Turf Installation Adelaide South — FAQs

Sir Walter Buffalo is our most popular recommendation for southern Adelaide due to its drought tolerance, ability to handle shade from neighbouring trees and fences, and its soft texture underfoot. Kikuyu and Couch are excellent options for full-sun areas where fast establishment is needed or budget is a consideration.
Ground preparation is the most critical part of any turf installation. Poorly prepared ground results in uneven lawn surfaces, poor root establishment, and long-term drainage problems. We remove old lawn, rototill the soil, amend clay soils with gypsum and organic matter, level carefully, and apply starter fertiliser before any turf goes down.
Spring and autumn are ideal for turf installation in Adelaide — mild temperatures reduce establishment stress and the need for intensive watering. Summer installations are possible with adequate irrigation but require careful daily watering management during the first few weeks. We're happy to install year-round with the right aftercare plan in place.
We recommend keeping foot traffic to a minimum for the first two to three weeks after installation to allow roots to knit into the soil. Light use can resume after about three weeks, and the lawn is typically fully established and ready for normal use within six to eight weeks.
