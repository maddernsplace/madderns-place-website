Seaview Downs Sloped Block Landscaping Transformation

About This Project

Turning a difficult sloping block into a practical, low-maintenance outdoor space.

The Project

This landscaping project in Seaview Downs transformed a challenging sloped yard into a functional and visually appealing outdoor area. By combining retaining walls, premium turf, irrigation, and garden edging, we created two usable lawn spaces while improving drainage, reducing erosion, and enhancing the overall appearance of the property.

The Challenge

The property featured a significant slope with multiple levels, making the yard difficult to use and maintain. The uneven terrain reduced functional outdoor space and created ongoing concerns with erosion and water movement.

The homeowners wanted a solution that would:

  • Increase usable lawn space
  • Improve drainage and erosion control
  • Create a clean, modern appearance
  • Reduce ongoing maintenance requirements
  • Provide a durable landscape suitable for family use

Our Approach

The project began with reshaping and preparing the site to establish two functional lawn areas at the upper and lower sections of the property.

Careful grading was completed to improve water flow and stabilise the sloped section between levels.

To maximise the available space and create a long-lasting result, we installed:

  • Timber sleeper retaining walls to support and define the change in levels
  • Metal garden edging to create crisp separation between lawns and garden beds
  • Premium loam soil preparation for healthy turf establishment
  • An automated Hunter irrigation system for efficient watering
  • Pine bark mulched garden beds to provide a neat, low-maintenance finish

Once the site preparation was complete, premium Kikuyu turf was installed across both lawn areas, creating durable green spaces well suited to Adelaide's climate.

Materials Used

  • 100mm Treated Pine Sleepers
  • Metal Garden Edging
  • Hunter Irrigation Controller
  • Premium Irrigation Components
  • Premium Loam Soil
  • Kikuyu Turf
  • 25mm Pine Bark Mulch

The Result

The completed landscape has transformed an underutilised sloping yard into a practical and attractive outdoor space.

The timber sleeper retaining walls provide long-term stability while creating clear, defined levels throughout the yard. The new Kikuyu lawns offer durable, family-friendly areas for everyday use, while the automated irrigation system helps maintain healthy growth year-round.

The result is a modern, low-maintenance landscape that improves both the functionality and visual appeal of the property while adding value to the home.
