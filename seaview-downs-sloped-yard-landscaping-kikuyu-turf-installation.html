Sloped Yard Landscaping & Kikuyu Lawn Installation in Seaview Downs

About This Project

This landscaping project in Seaview Downs transformed a difficult sloped block into a practical, attractive, and low-maintenance outdoor space. By combining timber sleeper retaining walls, premium Kikuyu turf, irrigation, and garden edging, we created two functional lawn areas while improving the stability and appearance of the property. The Challenge This Seaview Downs property featured a significant slope with multiple levels, limiting the usable outdoor space and creating ongoing maintenance challenges. The homeowners wanted a solution that would improve the functionality of the yard, reduce erosion, and provide a clean, modern landscape that would be easy to maintain. The Approach We began by reshaping and preparing the site to create two usable lawn areas at the top and bottom of the property while stabilising the sloped section between them. Careful grading and site preparation were completed to improve drainage and minimise future erosion issues. To manage the change in levels and maximise the usable space, we installed: Timber sleeper retaining walls to support and define the sloped section Metal garden edging to create clean separation between lawn and garden areas Pine bark mulched garden beds for a low-maintenance finish Hunter irrigation system for efficient watering Premium loam soil preparation to support healthy turf establishment Once the groundwork was complete, premium Kikuyu turf was installed across the upper and lower lawn areas, creating two durable and attractive lawn spaces suitable for the Adelaide climate. Materials Used 100mm Treated Pine Sleepers Metal Garden Edging Hunter Irrigation Timer Premium Irrigation Components Premium Loam Soil Kikuyu Turf 25mm Pine Bark Mulch Time Taken Approximately 4 days from start to completion. Project Cost Range Projects of this size and scope in Seaview Downs typically range between $3,000 and $5,000, depending on site access, excavation requirements, retaining wall specifications, irrigation needs, and material selections. Key Features Sloped block landscaping in Seaview Downs Timber sleeper retaining walls Metal garden edging Kikuyu turf installation Automated irrigation system Premium loam soil preparation Pine bark mulched garden beds Improved drainage and erosion control Two functional lawn areas created from a challenging sloped site The Result The completed Seaview Downs landscaping project transformed an underutilised sloping yard into a practical and visually appealing outdoor space. The timber sleeper retaining walls provide long-term stability while defining the landscape levels. The new Kikuyu lawns at the top and bottom of the property offer durable, family-friendly lawn areas, while the irrigation system helps maintain a healthy, green lawn throughout the year. The result is a modern, low-maintenance landscape that improves both the appearance and usability of the property while adding value to the home. Looking for Landscaping in Seaview Downs?
