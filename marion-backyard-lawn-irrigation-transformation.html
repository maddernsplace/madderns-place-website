Marion Backyard Lawn & Irrigation Transformation

About This Project

The Project

This backyard landscaping project in Marion transformed a tired, weed-infested yard into a clean, functional, and low-maintenance outdoor space designed for year-round enjoyment.

Through a combination of site excavation, weed removal, premium soil preparation, irrigation installation, and fresh Kikuyu turf, the property was completely revitalised and given a fresh new look that significantly improves both usability and presentation.

The Goal

The existing backyard had become overrun with weeds and lacked a healthy lawn area suitable for everyday use. The homeowners wanted a simple, attractive landscape that would be easy to maintain while improving the overall appearance of the property.

The project objectives included:

  • Removing existing weeds and unwanted vegetation
  • Creating a healthy and durable lawn area
  • Installing an automated irrigation system
  • Defining garden beds with metal edging
  • Improving soil quality and drainage
  • Enhancing the overall appearance of the backyard
  • Reducing ongoing maintenance requirements

The Process

The project commenced with the removal of weeds and existing vegetation throughout the yard. Once cleared, the site was excavated and reshaped to establish suitable levels and provide a clean foundation for the new landscape.

Premium loam was then imported and spread across the site to improve soil quality and provide the ideal growing environment for the new turf.

To ensure long-term lawn health, a fully automated irrigation system was installed using a Hunter Node battery-operated controller paired with Hunter MP Rotator sprinklers. This combination provides highly efficient water distribution while reducing water wastage and maintaining consistent coverage across the lawn.

Metal garden edging was installed around the perimeter of the lawn and garden beds, creating clean separation lines and helping maintain a tidy, professional appearance.

Once preparation was complete, premium Kikuyu turf was laid throughout the central lawn area, creating a durable and hard-wearing surface well suited to Adelaide's climate and family use.

Garden beds were finished with fresh mulch and selected plantings, completing the transformation and adding colour and structure around the new lawn.

The Result

The completed Marion landscaping project transformed an outdated and weed-filled backyard into a clean, modern outdoor space that is both practical and visually appealing.

The new Kikuyu lawn creates a large usable area for family activities while the Hunter irrigation system ensures efficient watering and healthy growth throughout the year. The metal edging provides crisp definition between lawn and garden beds, while the mulched planting areas soften the overall landscape and enhance the property's appearance.

The result is a fresh, low-maintenance backyard that is easy to care for, highly functional, and designed to thrive in Adelaide's conditions for years to come.
