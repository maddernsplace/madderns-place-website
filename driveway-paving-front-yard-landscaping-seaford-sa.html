Driveway & Front Yard Paving Transformation in Seaford, South Australia

About This Project

Creating a modern, practical, and durable entrance that enhances both street appeal and everyday functionality.

The Project

This driveway and front yard landscaping project in Seaford transformed an outdated and underutilised space into a clean, modern outdoor area designed for long-term performance. Using premium paving products and retaining wall systems, the project delivered a larger, more functional driveway while improving the overall appearance of the property.

The result is a low-maintenance landscape that maximises usable space and creates a welcoming first impression.

The Goal

The existing front yard lacked structure and consistency, with mixed surfaces that limited functionality and reduced the property's street appeal.

The homeowners wanted to:

  • Increase usable driveway and parking space
  • Improve vehicle access
  • Create a modern and cohesive appearance
  • Reduce ongoing maintenance requirements
  • Retain and highlight the established feature tree
  • Improve drainage and surface durability

A key objective was integrating the mature feature tree into the new design while ensuring the finished space remained practical and visually balanced.

The Process

The project began with excavation, site preparation, and construction of a compacted foundation to provide a stable base for the new paving.

Proper preparation is one of the most important stages of any driveway paving project, helping to prevent movement, settlement, and drainage issues over time.

Once the base was complete, premium charcoal concrete pavers were installed throughout the driveway and front yard area. Careful attention was given to alignment, levels, and finishing details to achieve a clean and professional result.

Special attention was given to:

  • Preserving the existing feature tree
  • Creating a defined landscape edge around the tree
  • Seamlessly connecting the new paving to the existing driveway and street
  • Establishing correct falls for drainage and runoff
  • Maximising usable parking and access space
  • Incorporating retaining wall elements for structure and visual appeal

Materials Used

  • Adbri Charcoal 300x300 Concrete Pavers
  • Adbri Versawall Retaining Wall Blocks (Charcoal)
  • Compacted Road Base
  • Bedding Sand


  • Concrete Edging Materials

  • Drainage and Installation Components

Project Duration

Approximately 4–6 days from commencement to completion, depending on weather conditions and site requirements.

Typical Project Cost

Driveway paving and front yard landscaping projects of this size typically range from $8,000 to $14,000, depending on:

  • Site preparation requirements
  • Excavation volumes
  • Drainage works
  • Paving area
  • Retaining wall construction
  • Material selections
  • Access conditions
