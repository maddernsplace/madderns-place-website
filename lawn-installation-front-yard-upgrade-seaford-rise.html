Lawn Installation & Front Yard Upgrade – Seaford Rise

About This Project
This project focused on transforming a sloped front yard into a clean, usable lawn space that complements the home and improves overall street appeal. The Goal The existing area lacked structure and usability due to the slope and uneven presentation. The aim was to create a smooth, level lawn that would be easy to maintain while tying in with the existing garden bed and retaining features. The Process We began by reshaping and levelling the area to create a consistent surface across the yard. Proper preparation is key to long-term lawn performance, so the base was carefully graded to ensure even growth and effective drainage. Once the foundation was set, we installed fresh turf across the entire space, working around existing features such as the raised garden bed and paved areas to achieve a seamless finish. Key Features New turf installation across a sloped front yard Careful levelling and grading for a smooth, even surface Integration with existing retaining and garden beds Designed for easy maintenance and long-term durability The Result The finished result is a neat, open lawn area that transforms how the space looks and functions. The clean lines of the turf soften the hard surfaces of the paving while complementing the surrounding landscaping. The yard is now far more usable, visually appealing, and ready to continue improving as the lawn establishes.
