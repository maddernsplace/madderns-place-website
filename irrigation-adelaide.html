What We Offer

Irrigation Built for Adelaide's Climate

Adelaide's Mediterranean climate — hot, dry summers and cool, wet winters — demands an irrigation system that's flexible and genuinely efficient. Overwatering is as damaging as underwatering, and a poorly designed system can waste thousands of litres each season while still leaving dry patches across your lawn or garden beds.

At Maddern's Place, we design and install irrigation systems tailored to your property's specific layout, soil type, and plant requirements. We use Hunter MP Rotators and pop-up spray heads for lawn areas, and Antelco drip emitters for garden beds — components trusted by landscapers across Australia for their reliability and water efficiency.

We also install Hunter Bluetooth NODE timers, allowing you to control watering schedules directly from your phone. Program multiple zones, set seasonal adjustments, and monitor your system without ever manually turning a dial — perfect for managing Adelaide's unpredictable spring and summer conditions.

Local Knowledge

Why Professional Irrigation Matters in Adelaide

Adelaide's southern suburbs present distinct irrigation challenges. The clay soils common in areas like Hackham, Morphett Vale, and Reynella have low permeability — meaning water sits on the surface rather than absorbing quickly. A standard spray system applying water at high rates will create runoff and muddy patches rather than the healthy, even absorption your lawn and garden need.

Our system designs account for this by specifying the correct application rate for each zone. We use Hunter MP Rotators with lower precipitation rates on lawn areas, and Antelco drip emitters in garden beds where slower, targeted delivery is far more effective. The result is a system that actually works with Adelaide's soil rather than fighting against it.

We also design around SA Water's watering day restrictions, which specify different permitted watering days depending on your suburb. Your controller will be programmed to comply with current restrictions from the moment your system is switched on — no guesswork, no fines.

A professionally designed irrigation system also protects your investment in turf, plants, and landscaping. Too little water stresses plants and kills newly laid turf within weeks — too much creates fungal conditions, root rot, and wasted water bills. Getting the balance right from installation day saves thousands in plant replacements and water costs across the life of your system.
