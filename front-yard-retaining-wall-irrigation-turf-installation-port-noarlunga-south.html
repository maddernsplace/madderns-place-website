Front Yard Retaining Wall, Irrigation & Turf Installation – Port Noarlunga South

About This Project

Transforming a bare new-build block into a functional, low-maintenance front yard takes more than simply laying turf. This project in Port Noarlunga South involved extensive site preparation, retaining wall construction, irrigation installation and premium turf establishment to create a landscape that will thrive for years to come.

The Challenge

Like many newly completed homes, the front yard had been left with compacted clay, uneven ground and a considerable amount of builders' waste scattered throughout the site. Large chunks of concrete, rubble and excess building materials needed to be removed before any landscaping could begin.

The block also sloped down towards the street, meaning the client wanted a retaining wall that would create a level lawn area while improving the overall street appeal.

The goal was to create something modern, practical and easy to maintain that complemented the home's contemporary design.

Site Preparation

Every successful landscaping project starts with proper preparation.

We began by removing builders' rubble, concrete waste and unwanted material throughout the property before reshaping the entire front yard.

The site was then excavated to the correct finished levels, allowing us to:

  • Remove contaminated and unsuitable soil
  • Create proper drainage falls
  • Prepare foundations for the retaining wall
  • Establish the correct height for the new lawn

Taking the time to prepare the base correctly ensures the finished landscape remains stable long after installation.


VersaWall Charcoal Retaining Wall

To overcome the slope, we installed a modern VersaWall retaining wall in Charcoal.

The wall follows the natural curve of the driveway before extending across the front boundary, creating a large level platform for the new lawn while adding strong architectural lines that perfectly match the home's dark roof, garage door and brickwork.

The retaining wall provides several benefits:

  • Creates a larger usable lawn area
  • Prevents soil movement
  • Defines the front garden
  • Adds visual depth to the property
  • Complements the modern façade

The gentle curves soften the landscape while maintaining a clean, contemporary appearance.


Hunter Irrigation System

To ensure the new lawn receives consistent watering, we installed a fully automatic irrigation system using Hunter MP Rotator sprinklers.

Hunter MP Rotators were selected because they provide:

  • Excellent water efficiency
  • Even coverage across the lawn
  • Reduced runoff
  • Lower water usage compared with conventional spray heads
  • Reliable long-term performance

The irrigation system services both the front and rear lawns.

Control is handled by a Hunter Bluetooth (BT) Node controller, allowing the homeowner to conveniently manage watering schedules directly from their smartphone without needing a mains-powered controller.

This setup is ideal for modern homes wanting reliable irrigation with minimal maintenance.


Premium Soil Preparation

Rather than laying turf directly onto the existing site soil, we imported premium landscaping loam to provide an ideal growing medium.

Premium loam offers:

  • Better root development
  • Improved drainage
  • Increased nutrient availability
  • Faster turf establishment
  • Healthier long-term lawn growth

Proper soil preparation is one of the most important investments in any new lawn installation.


Kikuyu Turf Installation

Once the site was levelled and irrigated, we installed premium Kikuyu turf across both the front and rear yards.

Kikuyu is one of Adelaide's most popular lawn varieties because it offers:

  • Excellent drought tolerance
  • Fast establishment
  • Strong self-repairing growth
  • Bright green colour throughout the growing season
  • Ideal performance for family homes

The lawn was carefully rolled, watered and finished to encourage rapid root establishment.


The Finished Result

The transformation completely changed the appearance of the property.

What started as an uneven construction site became a clean, modern and functional landscape featuring:

  • Premium Kikuyu lawn
  • VersaWall Charcoal retaining wall
  • Fully automated Hunter irrigation
  • Professionally prepared premium loam
  • Clean grading and improved street appeal

The raised lawn now frames the home beautifully while providing a practical outdoor space that's easy to maintain.
